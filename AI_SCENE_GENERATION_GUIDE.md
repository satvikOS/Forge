# AI-Powered Scene Generation - Quick Start Guide

## What's New?

ARchDisc now uses **Gemini AI** with **real-world geographical data** to generate intelligent, realistic 3D scenes from natural language prompts.

## How It Works

1. **You describe** what you want: "medieval village with church"
2. **AI analyzes** your prompt using comprehensive taxonomy
3. **Real-world data** applies authentic urban planning patterns
4. **3D scene generates** with realistic placement and spacing
5. **Unique every time** - same prompt creates variations

## What Makes It Intelligent?

### Real-World Urban Patterns
Your scenes use authentic city planning principles:
- **Manhattan Grid**: Dense orthogonal streets (New York style)
- **Medieval Organic**: Irregular winding streets (European style)
- **Suburban Dendritic**: Cul-de-sacs and hierarchical roads
- **Coastal Linear**: Buildings along waterfront

### Realistic Building Densities
Based on real cities:
- **CBD**: 40 buildings/hectare, 100-400m tall
- **Urban**: 80 buildings/hectare, 15-60m tall
- **Suburban**: 15 buildings/hectare, 5-12m tall
- **Rural**: 5 buildings/hectare, 4-10m tall

### Authentic Vegetation Patterns
From satellite imagery analysis:
- **Urban Parks**: 100 trees/hectare, mixed species
- **Forests**: 800 trees/hectare, continuous canopy
- **Street Trees**: Linear placement, 10-12m spacing
- **Coastal**: Palm trees clustered near water

### Proper Spatial Relationships
- Buildings placed on flat ground
- Roads connect buildings logically
- Vehicles on roads, not random
- Trees in natural clusters
- Water at appropriate elevations

## Understanding the Results

### What You'll See

Each generated scene includes:
- **Primary Elements**: Main buildings/structures (placed first)
- **Secondary Elements**: Roads, infrastructure (connect primary)
- **Tertiary Elements**: Vegetation, decorations (fill spaces)

### Variations
Even identical prompts create unique scenes:
- Different building positions

- Seed-based uniqueness

## What's Analyzed

When you submit a prompt, AI extracts:

1. **Primary Category**: Settlement, building, environment, etc.
2. **Scale**: From isolated dwelling to megalopolis
3. **Style**: Modern, medieval, futuristic, etc.
4. **Elements**: Specific buildings, terrain, vegetation
5. **Spatial Layout**: How to arrange everything
6. **Environmental Context**: Terrain, water, climate

## Real-World Data Applied

Your scene gets enhanced with:
- **Urban planning standards** (block sizes, street widths)
- **Building density calculations** (FAR, lot coverage)
- **Road network patterns** (grid, radial, organic)
- **Vegetation distribution** (tree species, clustering)
- **Terrain profiles** (elevation, slopes)
- **Spatial metrics** (walkability, green space)

## Troubleshooting

### Scene looks too sparse
→ Use larger settlement type: "city" instead of "village"

### Buildings overlap
→ This shouldn't happen! Report if it does.

### Not enough variety
→ Try more specific prompts with features mentioned

### Generation slow
→ Reduce complexity: "small town" instead of "megalopolis"
